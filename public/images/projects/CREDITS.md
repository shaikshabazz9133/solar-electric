# Case study photography

Placeholder photography for the six project cards, pending NorthStar's own job
photos. Every frame is licensed for commercial use. Cropped to 16:10 and
re-encoded at 1400×875; otherwise unaltered.

These stand in for jobs NorthStar has not photographed yet — they show the right
kind of work, not the specific site named on the card. Replace them (same names,
same 16:10 crop) with the real job photos and this file can be deleted; the
paths live on `image` in `src/lib/data/projects.ts`, one per project slug.

Share-alike (CC BY-SA) images were deliberately avoided: cropping one produces a
derivative that would have to be published under the same licence.
